## Why

VisioTrade reports `Earnings provider "financialdatasets" does not supply upcoming report dates`. A check against `https://market-data-hub.framas.cloud` on 18 November 2026 confirmed that this is the designed behaviour: Financial Datasets only delivers reported earnings (`earnings_upcoming = False`, `GET /earnings/next` answers 501), Alpha Vantage, the only provider with upcoming dates, is not enabled on the server, and no `default_earnings_provider` is set (`GET /earnings/next` without `provider` answers 503). Upcoming report dates are what VisioTrade needs most: an earnings release inside the DTE is the most common reason to skip a short-premium trade.

EarningsAPI (`https://api.earningsapi.com`, docs `https://www.earningsapi.com/docs`) has a free tier (60 requests per minute, 100 per day, 1,000 per month, quotas reset in New York time, 24 months of history, values updated once daily) and two matching endpoints:

- `GET /v2/earnings?symbol=ADI` returns the upcoming report and the reported quarters of one symbol in one request: report date, release time (`time-pre-market`, `time-after-hours` or `null`), EPS estimate and actual, revenue estimate and actual.
- `GET /v2/calendar/earnings?date=2026-11-24` returns all companies of the market reporting on one date, grouped into `pre`, `after` and `notSupplied`, with the same values.

The per-symbol endpoint gives history and dates further ahead; the day calendar refreshes the next days and the actuals of the last report day for every symbol with one request per day. Together they keep a watchlist current within the free tier. There is no subscription cost, which matches the preference for free or pay-per-request providers.

## What Changes

- New provider **EarningsAPI** (`earningsapi`, free key, `apikey` query parameter) implementing the earnings capability with both endpoints: per-symbol events from `/v2/earnings` and a market-wide day calendar from `/v2/calendar/earnings`. API key in the encrypted key store, connection test, request pacing for 60 per minute, request budget defaults 100 per day and 1,000 per month.
- **Two earnings interfaces, one capability each** (like every other MDH capability): `BaseEarningsUpcomingProvider` (`earnings_upcoming`: announced dates and estimates; mode `calendar` or `symbol`, optional day calendar) and `BaseEarningsHistoryProvider` (`earnings_history`: reported quarters). Alpha Vantage and EarningsAPI implement both, Financial Datasets only history. `earnings` stays in the catalog as the union.
- **Day-calendar window** for providers with a day calendar: the service keeps the weekdays from the previous trading day through `EARNINGS_DAY_CALENDAR_WEEKDAYS` (default 7) weekdays ahead synchronised in the store. Each day is fetched once; yesterday, today and the next two weekdays are refreshed when their last synchronisation is older than 20 hours. A day calendar is authoritative for its date for that provider. About five requests per weekday, about 110 per month, for the whole market.
- **Per-symbol refresh** for `symbol` providers: a stock is requested only when the request needs data the store lacks — it has never been synchronised (and, for `/earnings/next`, the day-calendar window does not already hold its next event), or it has no upcoming event stored and has reported since its last synchronisation (once per quarter, for the next quarter's date), or it has no announced date and its last synchronisation is older than `EARNINGS_SYMBOL_RESYNC_DAYS` (default 7), or a day calendar removed its stored event. A stock with a known next date is not refetched for its age; moved dates are caught by the day-calendar window. Actual EPS of recent reports comes from the day calendar, not from per-symbol requests. Symbols beyond the remaining budget are served from the store or get `status: "error"` with a budget message, in request order.
- A per-symbol synchronisation replaces that provider's stored upcoming events of the symbol; a day-calendar synchronisation replaces all of that provider's events of the date and drops a symbol's other upcoming events within 60 days of it. Moved report dates therefore leave no stale events.
- `EarningsEvent` gets the optional fields `revenue_estimate` and `revenue_actual` (Decimal, currency of the event); stored, returned by the HTTP endpoints, CSV export and MCP tools. Other providers leave them `null`.
- New query parameter `kind` (`all` default, `upcoming`, `reported`) for `GET /earnings/calendar`, `GET /companies/{symbol}/earnings` and the MCP tool `get_earnings_calendar`, so that clients such as VisioTrade can read forward (upcoming) events separately from reported ones; `kind=upcoming` requests no history, `kind=reported` no calendar.
- **Role defaults**: `default_earnings_upcoming_provider` and `default_earnings_history_provider` replace `default_earnings_provider` (migrated once, still accepted as shorthand). `/earnings/next` and `kind=upcoming` use the upcoming role, `kind=reported` the history role, `kind=all` both (unreported events from the upcoming provider, reported events from the history provider, report-day duplicates removed, `meta.providers`). `GET /providers` lists `available.earnings_upcoming`, `available.earnings_history` and the defaults incl. the missing `defaults.earnings`; admin shows two dropdowns and per-interface chips; a 501 for a missing role names the providers that have it. Clients such as VisioTrade choose their forward provider from `earnings_upcoming`.
- Admin UI: key field, budget settings and usage display for `earningsapi` like the other metered providers.
- Operation: after deployment, enable `earningsapi` on `market-data-hub.framas.cloud` and set it as upcoming default (and as history default, or keep `financialdatasets` for history), so that VisioTrade can call `/earnings/next` without `provider`.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `earningsapi-provider`: EarningsAPI integration for per-symbol events and the day calendar, credentials, connection test, error mapping, pacing and request budget defaults.

### Modified Capabilities
- `earnings-calendar`: two earnings interfaces with role resolution and composed responses, roles in the provider catalog, upcoming modes and day calendar, day-calendar window, per-symbol refresh, replacement rules for moved dates, revenue fields, `kind` filter.
- `system-defaults`: `earnings_upcoming`/`earnings_history` categories, two earnings defaults replacing `default_earnings_provider` (with migration), two dropdowns.
- `provider-capabilities-display`: `Earnings upcoming` and `Earnings history` chips.

## Impact

- **Code**: new `app/providers/earningsapi.py`; `app/providers/base.py` (`BaseEarningsUpcomingProvider`, `BaseEarningsHistoryProvider`), `app/providers/alphavantage.py` and `app/providers/financialdatasets.py` (implement their interfaces), `app/admin/` system settings (two defaults, migration), `app/services/earnings_service.py` and `app/services/earnings_planner.py` (day window and per-symbol plan), `app/models/earnings.py` (revenue fields), `app/store/` (Alembic migration for the revenue columns, day scopes `calendar-day:YYYY-MM-DD` in the existing sync table, replacement rules), `app/providers/registry.py`, `app/admin/provider_config_repository.py` (`KNOWN_PROVIDERS`, budget defaults), `app/api/routes_admin.py` (credential allowlist, capabilities incl. `earnings_upcoming`), `app/api/routes_meta.py` (`/providers` catalog), `app/observability/metrics.py`, `app/api/mcp.py` (tool descriptions), `app/config.py` (`EARNINGS_DAY_CALENDAR_WEEKDAYS`, `EARNINGS_SYMBOL_RESYNC_DAYS`), Admin UI.
- **API**: additive (`available.earnings_upcoming`, `available.earnings_history`, `defaults.*`, `meta.providers`, `details.supported` on the 501); admin settings: `default_earnings_provider` replaced by two settings (accepted as shorthand). New optional response fields `revenue_estimate` and `revenue_actual`; new provider name `earningsapi`.
- **Dependencies**: none.
- **Costs**: none (free tier). The per-symbol request is needed about once per quarter and symbol (after the report, for the next date), plus weekly checks while a date is not announced; the day calendar costs about 110 requests per month regardless of the watchlist size. With 100 symbols: about 100 for the first synchronisation, then about 150–280 per month of 1,000. How often VisioTrade or TradeSensei call MDH does not change this; MDH serves them from its store.
- **Not covered**: forward EPS consensus per fiscal year (forward P/E; EarningsAPI only carries the next quarter's estimate), day-calendar backfill of history (the per-symbol endpoint covers history), skipping market holidays in the window (they cost one empty request each), EarningsAPI's other endpoints (release times, reactions, movers, insider trades, profile, economic calendar), history older than 24 months on the free tier, a background scheduler, changes to Alpha Vantage or Financial Datasets beyond declaring their upcoming source, and VisioTrade changes.
